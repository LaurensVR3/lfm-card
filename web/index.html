LFM Card

A live driver card for your stream. It shows your LFM rating, licence and safety rating, counts down to your next race, shows your result and championship position afterwards and celebrates wins, podiums and promotions. It's free, needs no install or account, and refreshes its LFM data every two minutes.

Step 1

Build your URL

Paste your LFM profile link or driver ID, pick a layout, and copy the URL into OBS. Your ID is the number in your profile address, e.g. lowfuelmotorsport.com/profile/99685.

Already have an overlay URL? Paste the whole thing into the ID box to load its settings and change them. The builder also remembers your last settings in this browser.

Step 2

Add it to OBS

Quickest setup: click Download OBS scenes in the builder and import the file in OBS under Scene Collection → Import. You get four scenes with every source sized and set up, including scene switching. Your current scenes stay in their own collection. Then add your microphone and webcam, and check the game capture picks up your sim.

Or add a source by hand:

  1. In the scene you want, click + under Sources and choose Browser.
  2. Paste your URL and set Width and Height to the size for your layout and stream (see the table below). Leave the default Custom CSS as it is; the background is already transparent.
  3. Tick Refresh browser when scene becomes active. Every time you switch to the scene the card fades in again and replays any celebration for your last race.
  4. Position and scale the source like any other. The overlay is drawn at the source size, so it stays sharp at 1080p, 1440p or 4K. A wider source (21:9, 32:9) gets a wider layout with more on screen.
  5. Optional: for automatic scene switching (starting soon and lower third), set Page permissions to Advanced access to OBS and name your scenes LFM Starting soon and LFM Racing, or enter your own names in the builder. To hear the celebration sound in the OBS mixer, tick Control audio via OBS.

What it does

Features

The card switches between four states based on your LFM data. You don't have to do anything; sign up for a race on LFM and the card follows.

Next race

When you're signed up for a race: series, split, driver count and a live countdown. The details row rotates through track, car, door number, your seed in class, SOF, weather and session lengths, and a scrolling list of the drivers around you in the field.

Official broadcast

On the starting soon scene: when LFM streams your race, a badge shows where to watch. YouTube broadcasts are scheduled ahead, so they show up before the race; the official LFM Twitch channels (EN, DE, ES) show once they're live. Drivers in your split who are streaming get a red dot in the entry list.

Team races

In endurance and driver-swap races the overlay shows your team name, the drivers sharing your car (on the card and starting soon scene; the smaller layouts show your car's door number and team) and an entry list of teams, and your door, seed and result are the car's. Drivers from your LFM team in other cars are marked in the entry list. Long races stay live for their full length, 24 hours included.

Championship

Your position in the championship of the series you're racing, from LFM's season standings: your division position when the series has divisions, else overall, with your points. Team races show your team's position.

Rating trend

Two small graphs of your rating (in the sim on screen) and safety rating over your recent races, with the change over that time. Shown when there's no race coming up.

Scene switching

Starting soon and the lower third can switch OBS scenes for you: from LFM Starting soon to LFM Racing a minute before the server opens, and back when your result is in, for the celebration. It only ever switches between those two: on any other scene it leaves you be.

Racing now

From lights out the countdown becomes a pulsing LIVE tag. It stays that way until LFM publishes your result: up to three hours, or the race length plus an hour for longer races.

Race day frame

With the Race day layout the scene steps aside a minute before the server opens. A slim frame shows the session (practice, qualifying, race) with the time left and a progress bar, series, track, weather and SOF on top, and you, your door number, plates, car and the field along the bottom. When the result is in, the full scene comes back with the celebrations.

Result

After the race: start and finish position with places gained, rating change, SR change, incidents and your best lap, plus a form guide of your last 10 finishes. Shown for up to 7 days, or until your next race is coming up.

Livery flip

Every 30 seconds your profile picture flips like a coin to show your car in your livery, with the car name. This uses the livery screenshot from LFM, so it works when you race with a custom livery uploaded to LFM. Without one, it flips to the car maker's logo.

Results table

On the starting soon and race day result screen: the finishing order of your class in your split, with gaps and everyone's rating change. Your row is highlighted.

Demo race

Add ?demo=1 (or click Demo race above) for a made-up race that starts about a minute after loading, with your real profile: countdown, practice, qualifying, a 2-minute race, then a win with results and celebration, about five and a half minutes in all. Handy for setting up your OBS scenes on any day. ?demo=team (Team race above) does the same as a team race.

Celebrations

A full-width banner sweeps across the overlay after a big moment. It plays once when the result comes in, and again whenever the scene is reopened, for six hours after the race.

Your colours

The accent colour follows your licence: gold for Gold, purple for Legend, and so on. Prefer your own colour? Pick Custom in the builder or add &HEXCODE=FF0033 to your URL. Lavaro Racing drivers get the Lavaro logo, and other drivers get their own team logo.

Multiple sims

The card shows the rating and licence for the sim of your next race. With no race coming up it uses the sim of your last result, then the sim you've raced most. Supported: ACC, Assetto Corsa, AC EVO, AMS2 and RaceRoom. To always show one sim, pick it under Sim; races in other sims are then ignored.

Help

FAQ

My next race doesn't show up

The card only knows about races you're signed up for on LFM. Once you register, it appears within two minutes.

The livery flip doesn't happen

The car picture comes from LFM's livery screenshots, so it only exists when you race with a custom livery uploaded through LFM. With a default skin it flips to the car maker's logo instead. The livery is taken from your next race, or your last race when there's no next race.

How fresh is the data?

Everything reloads every two minutes, the same as the official LFM card. Rating, licence and results update as soon as LFM publishes them, usually a few minutes after the race. Championship standings reload every 30 minutes, and can take a few seconds to appear for big series.

Is the card blank or stuck?

If the overlay says it can't load your driver, check the number in the URL against your LFM profile link; the builder above checks it for you. If LFM is briefly unreachable the card keeps showing the last data it had and catches up on the next refresh. Right-click the source in OBS and choose Refresh to force a reload.

Do I need to refresh after an update?

No. The overlay checks for new versions every few minutes and reloads itself at a quiet moment: not during a race, not in the 15 minutes before one and not mid-celebration. If something still looks off, open the browser source's properties in OBS and click Refresh cache of current page.

What does "LFM data 12 min old" mean?

LFM couldn't be reached for a while, so the overlay is showing the last data it had. The tag disappears on its own once LFM answers again.

What does the form row show?

Your last finishes in the sim on screen, oldest on the left, latest on the right, from your LFM race history. Volt = win, volt outline = podium, light grey = top 10, dark grey = the rest. DNF means LFM has no finishing position for you in that race: a retirement, but also a race you qualified for and didn't start or left early.

Why doesn't the broadcast badge show?

It only appears on the starting soon and race day scenes when LFM is streaming your race. YouTube streams count when the title has your series and track and is scheduled for race day, e.g. [ENG] Caseking.de Prime Series R05 Suzuka. Twitch has no public schedule, so the official LFM Twitch channels only show once they're live. The YouTube schedule is checked every 10 minutes.

Can it show who's driving in a driver-swap race, or my live position?

Not yet. LFM doesn't publish live timing, so the overlay knows your car, its drivers, your team and the field, but not who is in the car right now or where it is running. It shows all the car's drivers instead and switches to your result as soon as LFM publishes it.

Scene switching doesn't happen

Open the browser source's properties and set Page permissions to Advanced access to OBS; without it the overlay may not touch your scenes. Both scene names must match exactly (default LFM Starting soon and LFM Racing, or set your own in the builder), and the source has to keep running when hidden: leave Shutdown source when not visible unticked. It only switches when OBS is on the starting soon scene a minute before the race (or on the racing scene when the result comes in), and decides once per race.

I can't hear the celebration sound

OBS plays browser audio to your desktop unless Control audio via OBS is ticked in the source properties; with it ticked the overlay appears in the audio mixer, where you can set its volume and monitoring. The downloaded scenes have it ticked. Turn the sound off with the Sound box in the builder.

Can I use more than one layout?

Yes. Each layout is its own browser source, so you can have the card on your intro scene, the lower third on your racing scene and so on. Build a URL for each one.

Does this page have to stay open?

No. OBS loads the overlay itself; this page only builds the URL. Your settings live in the URL, so you can paste it back in here later to change them.

Does it work for drivers outside Lavaro?

Yes. Any LFM driver can use it. You get your own team logo and licence colours.

The race day frame covers part of my sim's HUD

Pick Top only or Bottom only under Race frame in the builder (frame=top / frame=bottom), and untick Frame corners if the corner marks are in the way.

Is this an official LFM product?

No. It's an unofficial fan-made overlay by Lavaro that reads public data from Low Fuel Motorsport. All race data belongs to LFM.
