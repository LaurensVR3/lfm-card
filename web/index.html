LFM Card

A live driver card for your stream. It shows your LFM rating, licence and safety rating, counts down to your next race, shows your result afterwards and celebrates wins, podiums and promotions. It's free, needs no install or account, and updates itself every two minutes.

Step 1

Build your URL

Paste your LFM profile link or driver ID, pick a layout, and copy the URL into OBS. Your ID is the number in your profile address, e.g. lowfuelmotorsport.com/profile/99685.

Step 2

Add it to OBS

  1. In the scene you want, click + under Sources and choose Browser.
  2. Paste your URL and set Width and Height to the size for your layout (see the table below). Leave the default Custom CSS as it is; the background is already transparent.
  3. Tick Refresh browser when scene becomes active. Every time you switch to the scene the card fades in again and replays any celebration for your last race.
  4. Position and scale the source like any other. The overlay is built on a fixed canvas, so it stays sharp at any size with the same aspect ratio.

What it does

Features

The card switches between four states based on your LFM data. You don't have to do anything; sign up for a race on LFM and the card follows.

Next race

When you're signed up for a race: series, split, driver count and a live countdown. The details row rotates through track, car, door number, your seed in class, SOF, weather and session lengths, and a scrolling list of the drivers around you in the field.

Official broadcast

On the starting soon scene: when LFM streams your race, a badge shows where to watch. YouTube broadcasts are scheduled ahead, so they show up before the race; the official LFM Twitch channels (EN, DE, ES) show once they're live. Drivers in your split who are streaming get a red dot in the entry list.

Racing now

From lights out the countdown becomes a pulsing LIVE tag. It stays that way until LFM publishes your result, for up to three hours.

Result

After the race: start and finish position with places gained, rating change, SR change, incidents and your best lap, plus a form guide of your last 10 finishes. Shown for up to 7 days, or until your next race is coming up.

Livery flip

Every 30 seconds your profile picture flips like a coin to show your car in your livery, with the car name. This uses the livery screenshot from LFM, so it works when you race with a custom livery uploaded to LFM.

Celebrations

A full-width banner sweeps across the overlay after a big moment. It plays once when the result comes in, and again whenever the scene is reopened, for six hours after the race.

Your colours

The accent colour follows your licence: gold for Gold, purple for Legend, and so on. Lavaro Racing drivers get the Lavaro logo, and other drivers get their own team logo.

Want to see them before race day? Add ?celebrate=win (or podium, pb, promo, starts, or several separated by commas) to your URL. Use the Test a celebration button above for a quick look.

Multiple sims

The card shows the rating and licence for the sim of your next race. With no race coming up it uses the sim of your last result, then the sim you've raced most. Supported: ACC, Assetto Corsa, AC EVO, Le Mans Ultimate, AMS2 and RaceRoom. To always show one sim, pick it under Sim; races in other sims are then ignored.

Help

FAQ

My next race doesn't show up

The card only knows about races you're signed up for on LFM. Once you register, it appears within two minutes.

The livery flip doesn't happen

The car picture comes from LFM's livery screenshots, so it only exists when you race with a custom livery uploaded through LFM. With a default skin the card keeps showing your profile picture. The livery is taken from your next race, or your last race when there's no next race.

How fresh is the data?

Everything reloads every two minutes, the same as the official LFM card. Rating, licence and results update as soon as LFM publishes them, usually a few minutes after the race.

Is the card blank or stuck?

Check that the URL contains your numeric driver ID. If LFM is briefly unreachable the card keeps showing the last data it had and catches up on the next refresh. Right-click the source in OBS and choose Refresh to force a reload.

The details row stopped rotating, or something looks off after an update

Your browser or OBS may still be running an older copy of the overlay code. In OBS open the browser source's properties and click Refresh cache of current page; in a browser press Ctrl+F5.

What does the form row show?

Your last finishes in the sim on screen, oldest on the left, latest on the right, from your LFM race history. Volt = win, volt outline = podium, light grey = top 10, dark grey = the rest. DNF means LFM has no finishing position for you in that race: a retirement, but also a race you qualified for and didn't start or left early.

Why doesn't the broadcast badge show?

It only appears on the starting soon scene when LFM is streaming your race. YouTube streams count when the title has your series and track and is scheduled for race day, e.g. [ENG] Caseking.de Prime Series R05 Suzuka. Twitch has no public schedule, so the official LFM Twitch channels only show once they're live. The YouTube schedule is checked every 10 minutes.

Does it work for drivers outside Lavaro?

Yes. Any LFM driver can use it. You get your own team logo and licence colours.

Is this an official LFM product?

No. It's an unofficial fan-made overlay by Lavaro that reads public data from Low Fuel Motorsport. All race data belongs to LFM.
